Brand is the most untapped asset in most companies. The marketing model I grew up in is broken. Marketing is the most misunderstood growth driver there is. AI is changing how companies meet their customers.

I've seen marketing from three sides: the founder, the agency and the consultant. In global corporations and in ten-person companies. It showed me what marketing can do once it's connected to the right things, built on the right processes and given a seat at the right tables.

What I believe

The change I believe in

Lever one

Put the brand first.

Brand isn't a nice key visual, or a film in the right corporate design template. It shapes the product, the price and the way you go to market. And with that, what a company is worth. Most companies use a fraction of it.

Lever two

Wire marketing into revenue.

Marketing should be a system built into sales, not a department that executes. Marketing that sells, not marketing that decorates.

Lever three

Let AI rebuild operations.

AI doesn't just make the old work faster. It changes how a company meets its customers and how it runs.

Get all three right and you don't get a better campaign. You get a different company.
